Michael addition vs Robinson annulation

Updated 2026-10-19

Key takeaways
  • Michael addition is one step; Robinson annulation includes it.
  • Robinson annulation adds an aldol ring closure and dehydration.
  • Only the Robinson annulation builds a new ring.

Students often mix these up because the first step is identical. The difference is what happens next.

Michael addition

A nucleophile, typically an enolate, adds to the β-carbon of an α,β-unsaturated carbonyl compound. The product is a 1,5-dicarbonyl (when the nucleophile is a ketone enolate) and no new ring forms.

Robinson annulation

It starts with that same Michael addition, then an intramolecular aldol forms a six-membered ring, and dehydration creates a cyclohexenone. So a Robinson annulation is a Michael addition plus ring closure.

FeatureMichael additionRobinson annulation
Steps13 (Michael, aldol, dehydration)
New ringNoYes, six-membered
Product1,5-DicarbonylCyclohexenone

How to decide in an exam

If the question gives base and heat and asks for a ring, think Robinson annulation. If it stops at the adduct, or mild conditions are used, think Michael addition. See the mechanism guide for the full pathway.
