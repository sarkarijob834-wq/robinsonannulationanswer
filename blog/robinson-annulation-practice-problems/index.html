Robinson annulation practice problems with answers

Updated 2026-10-19

Key takeaways
  • Always write the 1,5-diketone intermediate.
  • The ring formed is six-membered.
  • The final C=C is conjugated with the C=O.

Work each problem on paper first, then open the answer. Use cyclohexanone and methyl vinyl ketone (MVK) unless stated otherwise.

1. Name the Michael adduct of cyclohexanone and MVK.

2-(3-Oxobutyl)cyclohexanone, a 1,5-diketone.

2. Which atoms form the new ring in the aldol step?

The terminal methyl carbon of the butanone side chain (as the enolate) attacks the ring carbonyl. The new ring contains six atoms.

3. What is the final product?

A bicyclic enone, 4,4a,5,6,7,8-hexahydronaphthalen-2(3H)-one, with the C=C conjugated to the new ketone.

4. Why does the aldol product lose water so readily?

Elimination forms a conjugated enone, which is lower in energy, and the E1cB pathway through an enolate makes the loss of hydroxide feasible under base.

5. Which reactants give the Wieland–Miescher ketone?

2-Methylcyclohexane-1,3-dione and methyl vinyl ketone.
