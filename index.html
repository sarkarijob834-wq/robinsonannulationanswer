The Robinson annulation answer in 30 seconds

A Robinson annulation joins a ketone (the donor) to an α,β-unsaturated ketone such as methyl vinyl ketone (the acceptor). The enolate does a Michael addition to give a 1,5-diketone. An intramolecular aldol closes a six-membered ring, and dehydration delivers a conjugated cyclohexenone.

Key takeaways
  • Michael, then aldol, then dehydration.
  • The new ring has six atoms.
  • The product always has a C=C conjugated to the C=O.
  • Always draw the intermediates for partial credit.

What is the Robinson annulation?

“Annulation” means building a new ring onto an existing molecule. The Robinson version builds a six-membered ring in one pot by chaining two classic reactions, a Michael addition and an aldol condensation. Because the product is a cyclohexenone, it is a favorite way to make fused ring systems.

A short history

1935
Robert Robinson and William Rapson report the ring-forming sequence.

1947
Robinson receives the Nobel Prize in Chemistry for his work on plant natural products.

1971
Hajos–Parrish and Eder–Sauer–Wiechert show proline-catalyzed asymmetric versions on diketones.

Step-by-step mechanism

Michael addition

Base forms the enolate. It attacks the β-carbon of the enone and gives a 1,5-diketone.

Intramolecular aldol

A second enolate attacks the other carbonyl, closing a six-membered ring and giving a β-hydroxy ketone.

Dehydration

E1cB loss of water makes the C=C conjugated with the C=O: the cyclohexenone.

Donors, acceptors and conditions

🧪

Donors

Ketones with acidic α-hydrogens. β-Diketones and β-keto esters enolize easily and react cleanly.

⚡

Acceptors

Methyl vinyl ketone is the classic. Substituted enones add steric hindrance.

🔥

Conditions

Alkoxide or hydroxide in alcohol, with heat to drive dehydration. Stork enamine versions run milder.

Worked examples

1️⃣

Cyclohexanone + MVK

Michael adduct: 2-(3-oxobutyl)cyclohexanone. After aldol and dehydration: 4,4a,5,6,7,8-hexahydronaphthalen-2(3H)-one.

2️⃣

Wieland–Miescher ketone

2-Methylcyclohexane-1,3-dione + MVK gives this bicyclic building block for steroids and terpenoids.

Common mistakes

  • Stopping at the aldol alcohol and forgetting dehydration.
  • Forming a four- or five-membered ring by using the wrong enolate.
  • Attacking the acceptor’s carbonyl instead of its β-carbon.
  • Placing the C=C away from the carbonyl.
  • Miscounting carbons in the 1,5-diketone.

Myths and facts

Myth
The Robinson annulation is just an aldol.
Fact
It starts with a Michael addition; the aldol is the ring-closing second stage.
Myth
It can make any ring size.
Fact
The classic reaction forms six-membered rings.

Regiochemistry and stereochemistry

Unsymmetrical ketones can enolize on either side. Thermodynamic conditions (alkoxide, heat) favor the more substituted enolate. Ring fusion in decalin-type products is usually trans under equilibrating conditions, though exceptions exist, so follow your course notes.

Practice problems with answers

1. Name the Michael adduct of cyclohexanone and MVK.

2-(3-Oxobutyl)cyclohexanone, a 1,5-diketone.

2. How many atoms are in the new ring?

Six.

3. Why does the aldol product dehydrate easily?

Loss of water gives a conjugated enone, and E1cB through an enolate makes it feasible under base.

4. Which dione gives the Wieland–Miescher ketone?

2-Methylcyclohexane-1,3-dione with MVK.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Robinson annulation answer in one line?

A Michael addition forms a 1,5-diketone, an intramolecular aldol closes a six-membered ring, and dehydration gives a cyclohexenone.

What is the product of a Robinson annulation?

An α,β-unsaturated cyclic ketone, specifically a substituted cyclohexenone.

Which base is used?

Commonly NaOH, KOH or sodium ethoxide, often with heat for the dehydration.

Who discovered the Robinson annulation?

Robert Robinson and William Rapson reported it in 1935. Robinson won the 1947 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for related work.

Is it one reaction or three?

It is a tandem sequence, often run in one pot, with three mechanistic stages.

Why a six-membered ring?

Six-membered ring closure is fast and the ring is low in strain, so the six-atom enolate attack wins.

How do I check my answer?

Confirm a six-membered ring, a C=C conjugated with the C=O, and that all atoms from both reactants are accounted for except one H₂O.

What is the difference from a simple aldol?

A Robinson annulation starts with a Michael addition, and the aldol step is intramolecular and forms a ring.
